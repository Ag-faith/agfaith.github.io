About Jim Abel

Four Decades Rooted in Agriculture

Jim Abel has been involved in ag real estate for over 40 years — as an appraiser, broker, and lifelong advocate for the farming and ranching community. His territory spans Central and Northern California, Southern Oregon, and Northern Nevada.

Most recently, Jim spent over 20 years in the Farm Credit system before establishing Ag Faith. His mission is to build a positive, motivated, faith-filled team to help farmers and ranchers prosper with Faith, Integrity, and Hard Work.

We provide both appraisal and brokerage services with a goal of instilling trust, compassion, and honesty with every person we serve.

What We Do

Farm & Ranch Appraisal and Brokerage Services

With experience across every agricultural property type imaginable, Jim brings unmatched expertise to every engagement — whether you need a certified appraisal or are buying or selling land.

I
Agricultural Appraisal

USPAP-compliant appraisals for farms, ranches, irrigated land, dry ground, timber, and specialty crops. Lender-approved and litigation-ready.

II
Ranch Brokerage

Buying and selling livestock ranches, recreational properties, and wildlife ground throughout California, Oregon, and Nevada.

III
Land Consulting

Market analysis, valuation opinions, and strategic guidance for ag landowners, investors, and lenders.

Jim Abel, ARA, MAI

40 Years of Agricultural Real Estate Expertise

Jim Abel has dedicated his career to agricultural real estate across the American West. With designations from both the American Society of Farm Managers and Rural Appraisers (ARA) and the Appraisal Institute (MAI), Jim brings the highest level of professional credentialing to every assignment.

His experience spans every type of agricultural property — irrigated farms, cattle ranches, timber land, recreational properties, vineyards, orchards, and wildlife habitat — across California, Oregon, and Nevada.
